Sneaker condition guide

A simple guide to help you understand each condition.

  • Like new

    Looks almost brand new. Very clean from top to bottom.

  • Excellent

    Worn a little, but still very clean and in great shape.

  • Great

    Used, but well looked after. Clean, nice, and ready to wear.

  • Good

    Shows more use, but still looks good and feels fine to wear.

  • Minor marks

    May have small marks or flaws. Any issues will be shown in the photos.
